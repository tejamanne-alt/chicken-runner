Chicken Runner

A village kid chases a smug rooster through a South Indian village of the 1970s. Catch him before he gets away.

Playing

Jump
Tap or Space. Hold for the full 1.4 m.
Double jump
Tap again in the air.
Slide
Swipe down or ↓. In the air it drops you fast.
Climb
Run into a ladder; jump at a branch.
Restart
R

The chase

The rooster starts 12 m ahead. Close the gap and the kid dives for him; let him get 25 m ahead, or to the end of the course, and he's gone. Each level has three stars: the catch, most of the mangoes, and no stumbles. Three tail feathers hide on the harder routes. Watch lets the autopilot run a level for you. This is an early build of a game being made for phones, so some of the art is still a stand-in.
