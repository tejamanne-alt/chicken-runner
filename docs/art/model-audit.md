# Model audit: every hand-built model, from every side

All 179 hand-built scenery models (everything but the people and animals, which stay painted cut-outs) were audited from the FBX the game loads: rendered like the game (toon light, ink outline) from the front in the painting's frame and compared with the painting, then from eight directions round it and from above, and measured (outline, holes, colour, streaks, flat faces, repeats, dark patches, floating and loose parts, inside-out faces, stretched or squashed texture, face budget). Eight agents, one per group of models, reviewed every sheet by eye against the 13-point checklist in [LEARNINGS.md](../../ArtSource/tools/kit/LEARNINGS.md), fixed the causes, rebuilt and audited again, three to twelve rounds a model. Every lesson went into LEARNINGS.md (and the agents' full notes into [learnings/](../../ArtSource/tools/kit/learnings/)), and every brief carried it in full.

## Result

- **9 passed as built**, **161 fixed**, **9 not yet perfect** (below). None passed by the numbers alone: each was looked at view by view.
- Every model was rebuilt with the final kit and audited once more for this report (179 audited). Front match below is that audit's IoU of the model's front against the painting (1.00 is a perfect match).
- **The models painted from above or in perspective** (19: the eight not yet perfect after the second round, bicycle, rope_cot, bullock_cart, crop_strip_2, pebbles, pebble_cluster, stall_e_3 and stall_h_2, and eleven with the same cause, the canal pieces, the field shrine, the granary, the drying mats, four more stalls and a tree platform) had a third round. The painter's view is measured from the painting and recorded on the model: how much higher things further back are drawn (`oblique`), how much smaller (`converge`) and how far sideways (`oblique_x`). The model stays true, level and square with every part at its real depth; the painting is projected along that view, so its tops land on the real tops, and the audit compares the front along it. Their fronts now match at 0.81-0.98 (pebble_cluster 0.10 to 0.96, canal_piece_2 0.28 to 0.91, rope_cot 0.62 to 0.87); where real proportions and the painting disagree (a stool painted 1.6 times real size), real proportions won.
